Features

  • Monitors and guides the sterilizing process.
  • Notifies the user when the incubation of a biological indicator is complete, allowing for timely verification of incubated BIs.
  • Prints autoclave-resistant identification labels with package contents and sterilization information.
  • Tracks, documents, and verifies the sterility of dental instruments to ensure full compliance with PIDAC requirements and PHO, CDHO, RCDSO, and ODA guidelines.
  • Fully customizable to individual office needs, including custom naming of an unlimited number of pouches and instrument kits.
  • Automatically connects and receives cycle information from compatible sterilizers.*
  • Allows the electronic transfer of all instrument sterilization information to the patient's file.
  • Generates sterilization compliance, management, and statistical reports as desired on the fly.
  • Securely and automatically stores and backs up sterilization data.

*Our optional data logger is an add-on that does not work with sterilizers that do not allow automated access to their cycle information. If you are interested in the add-on, email us the brand name and model of the sterilizers you use and we'll get back to you.

Frequently Asked
Questions

Which sterilizers do
you support?

All of them! We pre-configure the solution to work seamlessly with your existing sterilizers.

Our optional data logger add-on, however, doesn't work with all sterilizers because some manufacturers don't allow automated access. If you are interested in the add-on, email us the sterilizers you use and we'll get back to you.

How much space do I need
in my sterile bay?

We know sterilization rooms are tight. We're continually trying to save you space. We will find a setup that suits your needs. Our space saver unit only requires 11" width & 12" depth of counter space to support the printer and tablet.

What if I run out
of labels?

We'll send you more! All consumables (labels and printer ribbon) are FREE as a Verifi subscriber.

How easy is it
to really use?

It's called “Guided” for a reason! Our program monitors and guides the sterilization process, prompting the user each step of the way; freeing them from tedious and time-consuming record keeping or having to remember the remaining verification requirements of every load.

support?

What happens to my
